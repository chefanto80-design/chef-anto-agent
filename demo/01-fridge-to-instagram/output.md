## 2. Three recipes

### Recipe 1 — Sarmale leneșe (Lazy cabbage rolls, vegetarian)
**Balkan comfort · 40 min · Serves 4 · Easy**

- **From the fridge:** half cabbage (shredded), 2 cups cooked rice, 1 carrot (grated), red bell pepper (diced), half the dill, ½ cup Greek yogurt
- **Staples:** 1 onion, 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp paprika, salt, pepper, 1 tbsp tomato paste *(optional)*, 1 cup water or stock
- **Steps:**
  1. Sauté onion, carrot and pepper in oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add cabbage, paprika and tomato paste; cook 10 minutes until soft and sweet.
  3. Stir in rice and water, cover and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Finish with chopped dill. Serve with a spoon of yogurt on top.
- **Zero-waste tip:** chop the dill stems finely and cook them with the onion; save the cabbage core, sliced thin, for Recipe 3.
- **Swaps:** dairy-free: skip the yogurt or use a plant yogurt. Gluten-free as written.

### Recipe 2 — Dill & feta egg fried rice
**Fast · 15 min · Serves 2 · Easy**

- **From the fridge:** 1 cup cooked rice, 2 eggs, a handful of dill, 50 g feta, ½ red bell pepper
- **Staples:** 1 garlic clove, 1 tbsp oil, black pepper
- **Steps:**
  1. Fry garlic and diced pepper in a hot pan for 2 minutes.
  2. Add cold rice, press it flat and let it crisp for 3 minutes.
  3. Push to the side, scramble the eggs, then fold everything together.
  4. Off the heat, crumble in feta and lots of dill.
- **Zero-waste tip:** cold, day-old rice fries best, so this is the right home for leftovers.
- **Swaps:** vegetarian as written. Dairy-free: skip feta, add a squeeze of lemon.

### Recipe 3 — Cabbage & feta fritters with dill yogurt (lahanopita-style)
**Greek-inspired · 30 min · Serves 2–3 (about 10 fritters) · Medium**

- **From the fridge:** 2 cups shredded cabbage (plus the sliced core), 1 carrot (grated), 2 eggs, 100 g feta, dill, ½ cup Greek yogurt
- **Staples:** ½ cup flour, salt, pepper, oil for frying, 1 garlic clove *(for the sauce)*
- **Steps:**
  1. Salt the cabbage and carrot, rest 10 minutes, squeeze out the water.
  2. Mix with eggs, crumbled feta, half the dill and flour.
  3. Fry spoonfuls in oil, 3 minutes per side, until golden.
  4. Sauce: yogurt + grated garlic + the rest of the dill + pinch of salt.
- **Zero-waste tip:** keep the squeezed cabbage water for Recipe 1's simmering liquid.
- **Swaps:** gluten-free: use rice flour or chickpea flour.

**Ingredients rescued: 8 | Meals made: 3 (about 8–9 servings)**
